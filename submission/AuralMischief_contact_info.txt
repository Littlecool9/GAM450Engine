================================================================================
AURAL MISCHIEF : Rhythm Action Platformer
================================================================================

Aural Mischief is a rhythm action platformer where your attacks and the world is powered by the beat. You play as a guitar-wielding hero navigating a hostile world of platforms and enemies, all synchronized to a pulsing soundtrack. Assimilate with the beat to deal more damage, and feel the flow of the music through traversing the world.
